FAQ

How does the €2 deposit work?

You pay €2 when you borrow a cup and get it back in cash or to the CupCycle app when you return the cup at any partner café or return box.

Which offer should I choose?

If you take 20 or more coffees to go a month, the Commuter Pass (€2.99 per month, unlimited cups) costs less. Otherwise, pay €0.15 per cup. The calculator in the pricing section shows your cheaper option.

Can I cancel the Commuter Pass?

Yes, any time in the app. The Pass is monthly and personal, so it cannot be shared.

What is the environmental impact?

On average, a coffee in a CupCycle cup causes about 46% less CO₂ than a single-use cup and lid, according to a life-cycle assessment following ISO 14044, reviewed by an independent expert. A cup makes up for the CO₂ of its production after 18 uses. The cups are made of polypropylene and are recycled after 200 uses.
